# Agent Portal: Coach Guide & Script

## 📋 **Quick Reference: Why Our Portal Beats Indeed**

### **The Problem with Indeed (and other job boards)**
- 10,000+ CDL jobs posted daily = overwhelming noise
- No personalization - same jobs for everyone
- Expired listings mixed with active ones
- No fair chance filtering for background-friendly jobs
- Zero support or guidance
- You're on your own navigating scams and spam

### **The Opptek Agent Portal Solution**
- **Curated for YOU**: Only quality jobs matching your preferences
- **Fair Chance Focus**: Filter specifically for background-friendly employers
- **Fresh & Active**: Updated 3x/week with jobs that are actually hiring
- **Coach Support**: Your Success Coach is in your corner
- **Direct Apply**: One-click to real applications (no middlemen)
- **Safe & Verified**: We screen out scams and spam

## 🎯 **Coach Script: Introducing the Agent Portal**

### **Opening (Builds Trust)**

"Hey [Agent Name], I want to show you something that's going to make your job search WAY easier. You know how overwhelming it is searching on Indeed? Scrolling through thousands of jobs, wondering which ones are real, which ones will actually consider your background?

That stops today. I've set up your personal Agent Portal - think of it as your own private job board, curated specifically for you."

### **The Value Proposition (Addresses Pain Points)**

"Here's what makes this different from Indeed:

**1. It's Personal**
These aren't random jobs. Every single listing here is filtered based on what YOU told me you're looking for:
- [Reference their route preferences: OTR, Regional, Local]
- [Reference their endorsements: Hazmat, Tanker, etc.]
- [If fair chance: Background-friendly employers who give second chances]

**2. It's Fresh**
We update this 3 times a week. No 90-day-old ghost jobs. If it's here, they're actively hiring.

**3. It's Quality**
Our AI screens every job before it gets to you. We filter out:
- Scams and spam
- Bait-and-switch listings
- Jobs that don't match what you want

**4. You're Not Alone**
See that feedback button? Use it. Tell me which jobs you're interested in, which ones aren't right, or if you have questions. I'm here to help you navigate this."

### **Portal Walkthrough (Features & How-To)**

#### **Feature 1: Your Personal Link**

"I'm sending you a link right now - bookmark this on your phone. This is YOUR portal. Nobody else sees these exact jobs.

[Send the Short.io portal link via text/email]

When you click it, you'll see jobs we've hand-picked for you. Tap 'View Jobs' and you're in."

#### **Feature 2: Job Cards**

"Each job shows you the important stuff up front:
- **Company & Title**: Who's hiring and for what
- **Location**: Where the job is based
- **Pay**: Salary or hourly rate (when available)
- **Route Type**: OTR, Regional, or Local
- **Home Time**: How often you'll be home
- **Requirements**: What endorsements they need

Read the summary - we've already pulled out the key details so you don't have to dig through walls of text."

#### **Feature 3: Expanding Jobs**

"See that down arrow? Click it to read the full job description. This is where you'll find:
- Detailed benefits
- Specific requirements
- Company culture info
- Training programs

If it looks good, hit 'Apply Now' - that takes you straight to their application page."

#### **Feature 4: Fair Chance Filter** *(For agents with fair_chance_only enabled)*

"Notice you're only seeing background-friendly jobs? That's intentional. Every job here explicitly welcomes people with records, or we've verified they consider fair chance candidates. No more wasting time applying to places that auto-reject."

#### **Feature 5: Feedback System**

"This is HUGE. See those three buttons at the bottom of each job?

**'I'm Interested'** - Let me know this is a good match. I'll follow up with you about it.

**'Not a Good Fit'** - Tell me why it's not right. Too far? Wrong pay? This helps me find better matches.

**'Tell Us About This Job'** - Got questions? Already applied? Let me know what's happening so I can support you.

USE THESE. This is your direct line to me. The more you tell me, the better I can help you."

### **Addressing Common Concerns**

**"Why not just use Indeed?"**

"You can use Indeed too - I'm not saying don't. But here's the difference:

Indeed shows you EVERYTHING. 10,000 CDL jobs. You're doing all the filtering, all the research, all the guessing about which ones are legit.

Your portal shows you 20-50 QUALITY jobs, already vetted, already matched to what you want. It's the difference between drinking from a fire hose and having someone hand you a cold bottle of water.

Use both if you want. But start here - this is where I'm putting the best opportunities."

**"What if I don't see enough jobs?"**

"That's by design. We focus on QUALITY over quantity. But if you're not seeing enough:

1. Use the feedback - tell me what's missing
2. We can adjust your preferences (maybe expand your search radius, or add route types)
3. Remember, we update 3x/week - check back Monday, Wednesday, Friday

It's better to apply to 10 solid jobs than waste time on 100 mediocre ones."

**"How do I know these are real?"**

"Every job here came from:
- Indeed (verified employers)
- DriverPulse (CDL-specific board)
- Google Jobs (direct from company websites)

Then our AI vets them. We're filtering out:
- Duplicate postings
- Scam listings
- Jobs that don't match your profile

Are they 100% perfect? No job board is. But we're doing the heavy lifting so you can focus on applying, not researching."

### **Call to Action**

"So here's what I want you to do:

1. **Bookmark that link** on your phone right now
2. **Look through the jobs** - spend 10 minutes, see what's there
3. **Give me feedback** on at least 2-3 jobs - tell me what you think
4. **Check back Monday, Wednesday, and Friday** - that's when we add new jobs

And remember - I'm here. If you have questions, if something doesn't make sense, if you want to talk through a job, just reach out. That's what I'm here for.

Let's get you working. What questions do you have?"

## 💡 **Pro Tips for Coaches**

### **Maximize Portal Engagement:**
1. **Send it via text** - Higher open rate than email
2. **Follow up within 24 hours** - "Did you check it out?"
3. **Celebrate feedback** - When they use buttons, acknowledge it immediately
4. **Update preferences** - If they're not engaging, ask if the jobs are wrong and adjust

### **Handle Common Objections:**
- **"I don't see many jobs"** → "That's intentional - these are the BEST ones. Quality over quantity."
- **"I want to search myself"** → "Totally get it. Use this as your starting point - takes 10 minutes."
- **"I already applied to these"** → "Great! Use the feedback to let me know - helps me avoid duplicates."

### **Track Success:**
- Monitor click rates (you'll see this in Short.io analytics)
- Ask about applications - "Which jobs from your portal did you apply to?"
- Celebrate wins - "You got that interview from the portal job? That's awesome!"
